## 490E module Which power suppy to use M624 or M625A

_WBS RACKMOUNTING · Oct 15, 2013|46_

> I have some 490E Line modules that I am racking. Which power supply is correct to use :The M624 or the M625A?

— **Blueroom1**, Oct 15, 2013|46

> Hi.
>
> as the documentation indicates the M624 is an un-regulated supply giving you only +24v - http://wbsps.ca/PDFs/Ward-Beck%20Systems%20-%20M624%20-%20Power%20Supply%20-%20www.WBSps.ca.pdf - you require a bi-polar supply for WBS modules. You could force this one to work, but being un-regulated you are susceptible to noise form the electrical supply. Please use the significantly larger M625 series units. They are intended to be dead quiet, fire up an entire console, and work around the clock. The M624 is a little supply intended for bulbs in the CH ON, meters, relays, etc...
>
> Hope this helps a little bit.
>
> Regards,
>
> Warren

## Ward Beck M8212 VU/S  power supply solution questions

_WBS RACKMOUNTING · Oct 21, 2013|46_

> I Picked up a M8212. No one has prints on this.I spoke with Bob Murphy at
>  Ward-Beck. Its was custom made for NBC about 1988. They have no specs for
>  it. I am in the process of getting a power supply for it. Its an odd power supply at 22 volts.
> Now I have a extra M624. My thoughts were take the 22 volts from that ( The print in this site indicates that there is a 22v tap) and run it through a regulated supply board I have. That board would give me the Regulated +-22 volts. The regulated board does also have a voltage adjustment on it .
>
>  The function of the M8212 is to monitor audio from 6 input sources. it Has a Bank of Six Selector switches to do this.
>
> It also has a bank of 6 selector switches for the VU meter functions :
>
> Test Level, Stereo, L+R, L-R, L Cal, R Cal
>
> The Two Mystery Switches are Labeled DA IN and DA Out. My thoughts are this is to select direct audio in and out jacks on the front panel.
>
> Rear out puts are 39 Pin ENAC connectors.
>
> Main board is 14-148 Issue 3
